01 A WORLD IN THE BALANCE

A fragile peace.
A fractured world.
An unwritten future.

The setting is historical.
The possibilities remain open.

1933

Recovery is uncertain. Political movements compete for the future. Across the world, the old order is beginning to shift.

Pax Exilis places these tensions at the heart of a developing grand strategy experience. A country is more than territory on a map. It is an economy to sustain, a government to direct, relationships to navigate, and forces to supply.

Beginning on 1 January 1933, the project asks a persistent question: how do the decisions of a nation become the course of history?

07 BEFORE YOU GO

The current
state of play.

Can I download or play Pax Exilis?+

Pax Exilis is in active development. No public game download or release date has been announced. This website presents the prototype and its development story.

Is this the same project as Atlas 1933?+

Yes. Pax Exilis is the new name for Atlas 1933. Earlier builds and authentic development screenshots still use the original name.

Which systems are implemented?+

Source review confirmed prototype systems for the political map and province orders, public budgets, government and cabinet, diplomacy and negotiated peace, military forces and supply, research, construction, national focus trees, and campaign saving. Complete balance, content coverage, and performance are not established.

What is still being worked on?+

Performance, map presentation, interface clarity, balance, and content continue to evolve. Some optimization experiments have been reverted. The project does not claim a finished commercial release, verified multiplayer, or generative leader conversations.

How was this website’s imagery made?+

The gallery contains authentic V1.18.0 development screenshots. The cinematic office background is AI-generated concept artwork created for this website, not gameplay. Decorative atlas illustrations use public-domain Natural Earth geographic data with contemporary boundaries, not the game’s 1933 borders.
